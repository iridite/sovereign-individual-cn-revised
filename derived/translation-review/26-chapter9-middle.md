4. `11-chapter9.md:96`

英文原文要点：`Outside of access to good cigars, caviar, excellent orchestras, and the ballet`

现译：`除了上好的雪茄和鱼子酱、出色的交响乐团和芭蕾舞之外`

5. `11-chapter9.md:100`

英文原文要点：`even Castro himself would have been hard-pressed to secure a packet of decent dental floss`
